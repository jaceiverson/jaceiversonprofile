Super Tic Tac Toe

Nine games of tic tac toe in one. Win a small board to claim that square of the big board, then win three big squares in a row to win it all. Pass the mouse to a friend, or host a game and play them online.

Rules: Dead Square

How to Play

  1. X goes first and can play anywhere.
  2. The cell you pick sends your opponent to the matching board. Play the top-right cell of any board, and your opponent has to play in the top-right board.
  3. Get three in a row on a small board to win that square of the big board. A small board that fills up with no winner counts for nobody.
  4. Get three big squares in a row to win the game.

Dead Square

A won board stays open. If you're sent there, you still have to play there, even though the moves can't change who owns it. You only get to play anywhere when the board you're sent to is completely full.

Free Square

A won board is closed. If you're sent to a won or full board, you can play any open cell on any board that hasn't been decided yet.

Playing Online

Enter your name, choose Play Online, click Host a Game, and send your friend the link (or the game code). The host plays X and picks the ruleset. Your browsers connect directly to each other, so keep the tab open until the game is done.

Want to talk while you play? Once you're connected, click Join Voice and allow the microphone. It's opt in: your mic stays off until you click it, and you'll hear your opponent once they join too. Your mark glows green while you talk.
