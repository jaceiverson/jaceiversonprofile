A QUICK REMINDER

You’re backing camels, not owning them.

On your turn, take one action. Roll for an immediate 1 GOLD, take a leg ticket, place a spectator tile, or secretly predict the race winner or loser.

GOLD is game points. Whoever has the most when the race ends wins.

WELCOME TO THE TABLE

One turn. One choice.

Everyone starts with 3 GOLD. Camels belong to nobody; anyone can back any racing camel.

  1. Roll: reveal one unused die, move its camel, and immediately collect 1 GOLD. Keep the pyramid ticket as a record. A leg ends after five of the six dice. Roll rewards are already paid and are not added again at leg scoring.
  2. Leg ticket: take the next ticket (5, 3, 2, 2). At leg end it pays that amount for first place, 1 GOLD for second, or costs 1 GOLD otherwise.
  3. Spectator tile: place or move your cheer/jeer tile onto an empty space, excluding space 1 and spaces next to other tiles. A landing earns you 1 GOLD per camel in the arriving stack and sends the moving unit one step forward (cheer) or back (jeer). Jeering puts it underneath any destination stack.
  4. Finish bet: secretly spend a color card on the overall winner or loser. Each color can only be used once across both piles. Correct cards pay 8, 5, 3, 2, then 1 GOLD in submission order; wrong cards cost 1 GOLD.

Stacks & crazy camels

A moving camel carries everything above it and lands on top of another stack. Higher racing camels rank ahead. The grey die moves black or white backward: if only one carries racing camels, that one moves; if the crazy camels are directly stacked, the top one moves. Spectator effects follow the moving camel’s direction.

Legs, partners & the finish

Leg tickets are scored as a combined total, with money never below zero. After every player acknowledges the leg results, tickets, dice, spectator tiles and partnerships reset for the next leg. The next player starts the next leg. With 6–8 players, you may use your turn to pair with an available player; at scoring we automatically copy the best positive ticket reward from each partner.

The race ends immediately when any camel unit crosses either end of the track. Racing camels carried backward across the start rank last. Final leg scoring happens first, then winner bets, then loser bets. Most GOLD wins; ties share victory.

Online play uses the same PeerJS service and opt-in peer-to-peer voice as Rook. The host manages the rules and hidden bets. Rejoin using the same browser; keep the host tab open. Mic access needs HTTPS or localhost. Some networks may block peer connections.
